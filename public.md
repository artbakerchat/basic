## 1. Project Concept
* **Name**: Beeplex
* **Description**: An interactive bee foraging simulator where players control or manage a bee navigating through environments to scout, locate, and collect nectar, bringing it back to grow and sustain the hive.
* **The Catch / Core Mechanic**: The elusive "dream nectar" only happens during the 3:00 AM window. The player must be active around 2:45 AM and hit the collection button around 2:55 AM.
