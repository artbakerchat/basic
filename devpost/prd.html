Autonomous & Gaze State Machine

How player gaze, autonomous bee flight, and the 3:00 AM real-time event interconnect:

Interactive Screen States & Behaviors

State 1: Initial Arrival & Perched Bee

The scene launches with live system clock tracking and the colony ready for dispatch:

Nectar Slider
0 / 20 Nectar
System Clock
Live (e.g. 03:15 AM)
Hive Score
Honey: 0 | Dream: 0
Behavior: The bee starts comfortably perched at the central hive. The DreamEvent_Banner is inactive. Gaze raycasting is active immediately upon mouse movement.

State 2: Scent Beacon & Retargeting Rules

How the bee prioritizes player gaze commands in real time:

Mid-Flight Redirection: If the player looks at Flower A and then shifts gaze to Flower B while the bee is still traveling, the bee immediately dynamically recalculates its path toward Flower B.
Mid-Harvest Lock-On: If the bee has reached a flower and has begun its 2–3s siphoning cycle, shifting gaze will not interrupt the harvest. The bee finishes extracting the current blossom, then departs immediately for the newly targeted beacon.

State 3: Nectar Harvesting & Capacity

Managing cargo and inventory limits:

Hover Duration: 2 to 3 seconds of hover over blossoms to siphon nectar.
Yield & Capacity: Each harvest yields +5 nectar. The golden slider dynamically fills. Upon reaching exactly 20/20, foraging locks out and return state triggers automatically.

State 4: Hive Return & Deposit

Two ways to bring home the harvest:

Autonomous Full Return: When capacity hits 20/20, the bee automatically prioritizes returning to the hive, overriding flower gaze until unloaded.
Manual Early Recall: Looking directly at the hive casts a return beacon, recalling the bee early even with partial nectar (e.g. 5/20 or 10/20).
Deposit Feedback: Touching the hive resets the slider to 0/20, increments StandardHoney_Text, and readies the bee for the next command.

State 5: The 3:00 AM Dream Bloom Event

The core mystery event of Beeplex:

Duration: Exactly 45 seconds of real-world time (representing 3:00 AM – 3:30 AM in-game).
Visual Atmospheric Cues:
  • Global 2D lighting smoothly dims to a deep midnight blue.
  • DreamEvent_Banner illuminates: ✧ DREAM BLOOM ACTIVE ✧.
  • Dream blooms uncurl with a pulsating cyan Point Light 2D.
  • Rising bioluminescent spore particles float upward from open blooms.
Reward: Siphoning dream blooms yields rare Dream Drops that deposit into DreamNectar_Text.

State 6: Key Unity Edge Cases

Gaze on Empty Ground: If the cursor moves over rocks, grass, or empty space, no new beacon is placed; the bee continues its current directive without jittering or getting lost.
Depleted Flower Patch: A flower harvested to exhaustion closes or wilts; looking at it will not place a valid beacon until it recharges.
Event Timeout Mid-Harvest: If the 45-second Dream Bloom window expires while the bee is in the middle of siphoning a dream bloom, it finishes harvesting that single dream drop before the bloom recedes.

Acceptance Criteria (Checkable During Build)

  • ✓ Hovering over a flower patch visibly updates the bee's trajectory toward that flower.
  • ✓ Gaze shifts in-flight instantly redirect flight toward the new target.
  • ✓ Gaze shifts during active harvest allow the current 2-3s siphon to finish before redirecting.
  • ✓ Nectar slider and numeric label accurately update in +5 increments up to 20/20 max capacity.
  • ✓ Bee automatically heads home at 20/20, or returns early when player looks at the hive.
  • ✓ Reaching the hive clears carried nectar to 0/20 and increments Standard Honey or Dream Drops.
  • ✓ At 3:00 AM, the 45-second event triggers banner illumination, midnight lighting, cyan Point Light 2D, and dream spores.
  • ✓ Dream drops harvested during the event deposit into DreamNectar_Text.
