🐝 Beeplex

A real-time ambient bee foraging simulator with a rare 3:00 AM nocturnal event.

✓ Scope Approved

The Unique Kernel

Instead of manual arcade flight controls, the player directs the bee indirectly: looking at a flower patch casts an invisible "scent beacon" that triggers dynamic pathfinding and hovering over the flower to gather nectar.

✧ The 3:00 AM Dream Bloom Window:

The simulation hooks strictly into real-world local time. At 3:00 AM sharp, the nocturnal "Dream Bloom" illuminates, empowering the bee to harvest elusive Dream Drops.

Proof of Concept Boundary

Unity Canvas HUD
Clock_Text, DreamEvent_Banner, Nectar_Slider, and HiveScore_Panel wired to game state.
Scent Beacon Guidance
Gaze / target placement on flowers casts an invisible beacon directing the bee.
Dynamic Bee Pathfinding & Hovering
Smooth autonomous navigation toward the active beacon and flower hovering state.
Nectar Harvesting & Capacity
Harvesting blossoms increments nectar slider up to defined capacity limit.
Hive Deposit & Score Tracking
Returning to the hive unloads nectar to increment Standard Honey and Dream Drops.
Real-Time 3:00 AM Clock Event
Continuously reads local system time to trigger the 3:00 AM Dream Bloom banner and Dream Drops harvest.
Hive Expansion & Upgrades
Spending honey on new comb cells, hive capacity, and defense.
Multiple Bees & Swarm Dynamics
Managing a fleet of worker bees simultaneously.
Weather & Predator Hazards
Raindrops, wind gusts, and nocturnal insects.
Atmospheric Soundscapes
Ambient nighttime crickets, wind rustle, and low-frequency buzzing.
Accelerated / Simulated Day-Night Cycle
Cut in favor of strict, authentic real-world 3:00 AM time commitment.
Direct Manual Arcade Flight Controls
Cut in favor of the calm, autonomous scent beacon system.
Multiplayer / Online Leaderboards
Out of scope for a focused, local proof of concept.
